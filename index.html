How CyberKazi Started

CyberKazi started from a real struggle we saw every day.


People would wake up early, walk far to look for a cyber, only to find it closed, or full, or no power, or no network. Others would spend the whole day queuing.


Some lost money on transport just to go and ask for a small service like KRA PIN, HELB, or printing one paper. Students missed deadlines. Job seekers missed jobs. Parents were stressed.


That is why we built CyberKazi. So you don't have to walk, queue, or wait. Just order from your phone, we do the work, you pay with M-Pesa to download.


CyberKazi was built for everyone to access online services easily.
