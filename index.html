How CyberKazi Started

CyberKazi started from a real struggle we saw every day.


People would wake up early in the morning, walk far to look for a cyber, only to find it closed, or full, or no power, or no network. Others would spend the whole day queuing, waiting for their turn, while their work, school, or business was waiting.


Some people lost money on transport just to go and ask for a small service like KRA PIN, HELB, or printing one paper. Students missed deadlines because their assignment was not typed on time. Job seekers missed jobs because their CV was not ready. Parents were stressed because school work was piling up.


We saw that pain, and we asked ourselves - why should someone walk, queue, and waste a whole day for work that can be done on a phone in less than 10 minutes?


That is why we built CyberKazi. So that you don't have to walk. You don't have to queue. You don't have to wait. You just stay where you are, order from your phone, we do the work for you, and you pay with M-Pesa to download it.


CyberKazi was built for everyone to access online services easily.
